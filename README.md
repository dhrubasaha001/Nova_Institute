# 🌟 Nova Institute Portal

A modern, full-stack educational institute management portal built with TypeScript, React, Node.js, Express, and Next.js. Designed for hackathons and real-world institutional needs, it covers everything from login authentication to medical and academic services.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🔐 **Login System** – Simple login with redirect to dashboard
- 🏫 **Dashboard** – Unified navigation for all institutional modules
- 🛏️ **Hostel** – Room allocation, complaint management, and visitor logs *(Planned)*
- 📚 **Library** – Book tracking, digital lending, and seat reservations *(Planned)*
- 📋 **Attendance** – Digital attendance system with analytics *(Planned)*
- 🍴 **Canteen** – Digital menus, food pre-ordering, and feedback *(Planned)*
- 📆 **Events** – Registration, club announcements, and event timelines *(Planned)*
- 🏥 **Medical** – Health checkup booking, reports, and doctor schedule *(Planned)*
- 📝 **Examination** – Online exam info, results, and records *(Planned)*
